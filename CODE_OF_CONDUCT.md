# Code of conduct

InboxOS is a small project with a simple aim: build useful software together and make everyone who takes part feel
welcome doing it. This code of conduct applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other space
connected to the project.

## The standard

Be kind, patient and constructive. In particular:

- Welcome newcomers and assume good intent
- Critique ideas and code, never people
- Accept that others may see things differently, and that the maintainer has to make the final call
- Give credit where it's due
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam and self-promotion

Not acceptable, anywhere in the project:

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior of any kind, including on the basis of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
  gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems to [me@dylanlambertson.dev](mailto:me@dylanlambertson.dev). Every report is read and handled
confidentially.

Depending on what happened, the response may be a private note, a public warning, removal of comments or contributions,
or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. The maintainer decides what's appropriate and may act
without warning in serious cases.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
